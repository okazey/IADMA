CURIOSITY FORMULAS
CURIOSITY arrises with AUDIENCE QUESTIONS:
Will this validate or threaten my current beliefs related to my desires, pain, insecurities, fears, identity?
How will the character react to something extreme?
How will character overcome this impending high-stakes problem? Will the bad thing happen? How will this affect the character I relate to and care about?
Who will be the winner? Compeition of strength, intelligence, beauty, talents, etc
Why are others giving signs this mystery is important? (Like: withheld secrets, social proof, they all laughed untill x, etc)
Is everyone right or wrong about this belief/myth?
CURIOSITY ARCHETYPES:
The Fortune Teller
Tease the future:“This microwave will change cooking forever.”
The Experimenter
Peer-to-peer demos:“I used to do it this way… now I do this. Here’s how.”
The Teacher
Expert breakdowns:“Here’s how I built a $1M brand with one product drop.”
The Magician
Visual “stun gun”:Use visual surprise to stop scroll (e.g., wig gag → reveal).
The Investigator
Reveal hidden secrets:“This AI agent is doing your job right now, and no one’s talking about it.”
The Contrarian
Directly challenge norms:“Your branding sucks because your space sucks.”
