PERSUASIVE LOGIC LEAP
Clever defense of absurd logic using misapplication of logic that is clearly wrong but feels oddly persuasive for a split second. Coherant with parts of what was previously established. Making nonsense feel smart. Thread of truth is what keeps it funny instead of just nonsense.Other clever logical leap defenses include: double meaning, idiom literalism, personification, inversion, excuses that rebrand failure as strategic cause, following expected flow of a typical sentance, coherant with the previous sentance but missing the point.
"I started eating soap because the doctor told me to eat clean." (double meaning defense)
"I’m just trying to keep up with traffic, but there’s no traffic, that’s how far behind I am!" (missing the point example)
"Laptop sighs every time I click rendering." (personification example, laptops don't feel tired but make sense for a split second)
"I’m early for work, for tomorrows work." (missing the point example)
"Technically, stress burns calories, so I’m working out emotionally." (excuse example)
"Next time I sell a car I'm gonna put an IQ test on the order form to make sure I don't talk to an idiot who tries to lowball me." (unpractical defense example, logically it would work but absurd because IQ test isn't practical)
"We've run out of time! No, looks like time is still going!" (literalism example)
"I don’t procrastinate. I prioritize panic." (inversion excuse example, inverting procrastinate into prioritizing)
